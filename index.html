About these materials

The PSYC40960 tutorials repository contains slide sources, rendered presentations, and teaching preparation materials. The source repository is private; this public site contains selected student-facing slides and the assets needed to display them.

These materials support the tutorials; they do not replace module teaching or official university guidance. Referencing exercises deliberately include incorrect and invented examples for discussion.
